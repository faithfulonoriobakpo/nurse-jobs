Nursing jobs with visa sponsorship

What the sponsorship labels mean

Welcomes COS: the advert itself says applicants who need Skilled Worker sponsorship are welcome (NHS Jobs "Certificate of Sponsorship" section, or sponsorship mentioned in the advert).

Licensed sponsor: the advert doesn't mention sponsorship, but the employer is on the Home Office register of licensed Skilled Worker sponsors, so it can issue a COS. Worth applying and asking.

Not confirmed: no sponsorship wording and the employer name wasn't found on the register (names don't always match exactly, e.g. trading names). Check before applying.

Adverts that say sponsorship is not available are left out. Always confirm on the advert, as salary and other Skilled Worker rules still apply.
